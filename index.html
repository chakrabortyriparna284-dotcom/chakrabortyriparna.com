MISSION 02 // REALTIME SAAS NEXT.JS • CONVEX • STRIPE
bidrank

BidRank — Pay-to-Rank SaaS Leaderboard

Full-stack competitive SaaS leaderboard inspired by the pay-to-rank mechanic of Outbid.lol, designed for live product discovery.

  • Pay-to-Rank Mechanic: Founders submit their SaaS or product to a public leaderboard where the highest-paying product claims the #1 slot.
  • Dynamic Outbidding: Any founder can outbid another product's current bid via Stripe Checkout to immediately climb above them on the board.
  • Real-Time Reactivity: Instant leaderboard rank updates powered by Convex live subscriptions without manual page reloads.

Type-safe reactive serverless architecture documented in bidrank.md:

Next.js App Router — Full-stack TypeScript frontend & routing
Tailwind + shadcn/ui — Composable, accessible interface system
Convex — Database, queries, mutations, actions, realtime subscriptions & auth
Stripe Checkout — Payment sessions & verified rank-bid fulfillment
